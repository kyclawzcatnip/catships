📖 CATNIP WARSHIPS FIELD MANUAL & WIKI

🚢 Complete 100-Ship Warship Fleet

The Catnip Alliance operates 100 specialized naval combat vessels across 4 tactical roles:

  • 🗡️ Attackers: High DPS combatants equipped with heavy catnip cannons & torpedo salvos.
  • 🛡️ Tanks: Massive dreadnoughts and armor-plated hulls capable of soaking immense damage.
  • ⚡ Rushers: High-speed interceptors with boosted turn response and rapid-fire guns.
  • 🎯 Long Ranged: Siege cruisers, snipers, and aircraft carriers delivering long-distance air/missile strikes.

🐱 "The Salty Meow" Officer Quarters & Tavern

Recruit elite Cat Officers at the coastal tavern and assign them to ship crew stations:

  • 🧑‍✈️ Captain Whisker: Faster ability cooldowns (-15% per level).
  • 🔧 Chief Barnaby: Bonus max HP and faster repair rate.
  • 🎯 Officer Claws: Boosted cannon accuracy & critical hit damage.
  • ☸️ Helmsman Paw: Enhanced maneuverability, turn speed, and acceleration.
  • 📡 Scout Purr: Increased vision range and target lock speed.
  • ✈️ Marshal Mittens: Boosted fighter jet flight speed & strafing power.

🎨 Catnip Shipyard & Customization

Modify your fleet with custom paint schemes and specialized turret hardware:

  • Golden Cat Paint: Shimmering gold hull finish (1,000 Coins).
  • Cyber Neon Paint: Dark carbon hull with vibrant neon glow (2,500 Coins).
  • Heavy Siege Turrets: Maximum damage per shell with armor penetration.
  • Quad Rapid Flak: High fire-rate dual/quad cannons built for aerial defense.

⚓ Coastal Basin & Harbor Infrastructure

The Catnip Harbor provides vital mid-battle support and strategic harbor features:

  • Docking Slip Repair: Restores +15 HP/sec while docked inside the harbor slip.
  • Coastal Flak Battery: Automated 100mm coastal turret firing explosive flak at enemy invaders.
  • Resupply Station: Instant cooldown speedup (1.5x) for ability recharge.
  • Lifeboat Morale System: Rescue floating survivors for +50 HP, +25 Coins, and High Morale speed boosts!
